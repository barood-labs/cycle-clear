Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Cycle Clear collects no data. Everything you enter (period dates, flow, symptoms, notes, settings) is stored only on your device in the browser/app storage (IndexedDB). There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK. The app makes no network requests for your data.

Your data is never sold, shared, or used for advertising. If you clear the app or browser data or uninstall the app, your data is deleted. You can export a backup file yourself and import it again; that file is under your control.

The optional PIN hides the app from casual viewing; it does not encrypt data.

Cycle Clear provides estimates only. It is not a medical device and must not be used as contraception.
